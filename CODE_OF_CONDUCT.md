# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Argue about code, not about each other.

## What we expect

- Assume the person you are replying to is competent and acting in good faith
- Critique the change, not the author. "This breaks X because Y" is useful;
  "did you even test this" is not
- Accept that a maintainer may decline your change, and that a contributor may
  decline to keep working on one
- Respect the fact that most people here are doing this alongside a job, in a
  second language, or both

## What we don't

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning or discriminatory comments, publishing
other people's private information, and sustained disruption of a thread or
review. Sexualised language or imagery is not welcome anywhere in the project's
spaces.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages and code comments — and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Reporting

Email **hello@aexy.io**. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept
confidential. We will tell you what we decided.

Maintainers may edit or delete comments, close threads, or block accounts, and
will say why when they do.

This document is a short-form statement of the same expectations as the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
which is worth reading if you want the longer treatment.
